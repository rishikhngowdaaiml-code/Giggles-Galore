“The main reason why we decided to have multiple titles in the works–like three games in the works at the same time–was because we wanted to convey to the players that Konami is serious about coming back into the gaming landscape,” Okamoto said via a translator. “A part of the strategy behind that was to work on multiple games at once so that we can have an annual release of a new Silent Hill game ever year. That way, players will know that it’s not a facade. We’re serious about coming back into the gaming space and providing players with new games.”

The Silent Hill franchise had certainly been on ice for a long time prior to this recent initiative. PT was planned as a teaser for the unreleased Silent Hills game, and following its cancellation, it was nearly a decade before any new Silent Hill games were released. Perhaps Konami is truly making up for lost time, but if its upcoming games are as good as the Silent Hill 2 remake and Silent Hill f, fans have little reason to complain.
